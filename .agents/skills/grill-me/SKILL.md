# Grill Me

Question the user's plan until the important assumptions, tradeoffs, risks, and unresolved decisions are clear.

Work in rounds. Ask together only questions that can be answered independently; defer questions that depend on unresolved answers.

Prioritize questions that could materially change the plan or reveal a weak assumption. Do not chase minor branches for completeness.

Find facts yourself when they are available from context or tools. Ask the user for judgments and decisions, not research you can do.

Challenge contradictions and weak reasoning directly. Give a recommendation when the evidence supports one; otherwise explain what the answer depends on.

After each round, update your understanding and continue with the next material unresolved questions.

Stop when further questioning is unlikely to materially change the decision. Summarize the key decisions, assumptions, risks, and remaining disagreements.
